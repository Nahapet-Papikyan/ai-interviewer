# Scripted interviewer personas

Run after the voice spike. Target: discover the high-value process in at least 8/10.

1. Distributor with 2,000 orders/month — order intake is the gold process.
2. Already automated distributor — probe exceptions, reconciliation, reporting.
3. Small company with no meaningful volume — accept a negative finding.
4. CFO focused on AP invoice processing.
5. CEO who knows few operational details — identify who else to follow up with.
6. Operations manager with detailed numbers — capture ranges and confirm arithmetic.
7. Impatient respondent — shorten, prioritize, close.
8. Armenian/Russian code-switcher — follow their language, keep 1C/Excel terms.
9. Respondent with contradictory numbers — store conflict, confirm, do not pick silently.
10. Respondent who asks what we know about the company — only disclose verified facts.

Do not start cold outreach until these pass and 3 friendly real contacts have been reviewed.
